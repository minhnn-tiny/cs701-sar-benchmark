(~55 s) Why YOLO26? The reference trains 21 million parameters, LoRA plus heads, on top of a frozen 86-million-parameter ViT that sees the image in 16-pixel tokens, and its detection neck and heads start from scratch. We spent a similar trainable budget on a detector that is small enough to train fully: YOLO26-L has features at stride 8, and its neck and heads were already trained for detection on COCO. The classifier is one linear layer on the deepest feature. We screened the alternatives at equal epochs: a stride-4 level, the larger YOLO26-X, RT-DETR and ConvNeXt with a cascade. None beat YOLO26-L per GPU-hour. Giving the classifier the high-resolution maps raised F1 slightly but cost two mAP points, so we protect the detector’s features.
