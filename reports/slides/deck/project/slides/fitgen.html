(~50 s) This chart splits the score by class and by where the scene comes from. Blue is training images: the model fits almost everything, 89 mAP. Green is completely unseen scenes: 18.6. Orange, the val-like holdout, sits in between: 51. Classes whose test images mostly repeat training scenes, like airports, oil tanks and harbors, inherit their fit. Cars and ships generalise genuinely. Bridges and playgrounds fail both ways, which is where our remaining gap lies.
