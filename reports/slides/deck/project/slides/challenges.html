CS701 Team 5 · SAR multi-task learning · 16 / 17

(~50 s) To pull it together, each challenge got a specific answer. Tiny objects: a stride-8 COCO detector trained end to end instead of an adapted ViT, which moved us from +7 to +36 at once. Hidden labels and repeated scenes: a holdout that mirrors val, with leak-free and fit diagnostics, and decisions on three seeds; the gains kept their sign on val. Radar statistics: SAR normalisation and physics-based augmentation. Two coupled tasks: cross-task decoding at inference. Budget and noise: a 26-million-parameter model, the three-seed rule and weight averaging. Still open are bridges and playgrounds: next we would condition the detection head on the predicted scene class, give the fine features more context, and mine look-alike structures as hard negatives. The main lesson: read the data first.
