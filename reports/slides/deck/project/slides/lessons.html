CS701 Team 5 · SAR multi-task learning · 14 / 15

(~45 s) Five lessons. Reading the data first mattered more than any single trick: tiny objects and repeated scenes decided both the model and how we validate. A holdout has to mirror the leaderboard, and even a good one predicts the direction of a change better than its size: our decoding gained 0.6 locally but 0.3 on val. Seed noise is as large as most effects, so we decided on three seeds. Physics-based augmentation and the coupling between the two tasks gave free gains. And what remains open is detecting tiny objects that only context can disambiguate.
