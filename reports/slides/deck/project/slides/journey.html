CS701 Team 5 · SAR multi-task learning · 5 / 17

(~60 s) This is our path on the val leaderboard. Our first two designs followed the course baselines: adapters on a ViT or TerraMind, plus extra small-object branches. They plateaued at +5 to +7. The jump came from switching to a COCO-pretrained detector trained end to end: a Faster R-CNN with a stride-4 feature pyramid gave +18.5, and a cascade with longer training +36.8. That design also taught us that val rewards fitting known scenes. YOLO26-L matched it at 100 epochs with half the parameters, and then four steps took it to +45.9: SAR augmentation and longer training, a better SAR probability, cross-task decoding with weight averaging, and picking the best of eight seeds.
