(~55 s) Val and test labels are hidden, so everything is decided on held-out training images, and how you hold out matters. An earlier design of ours built a leak-free holdout with no repeated scenes. On it the reference scores half its val mAP, and two candidate models swapped places compared with val. So we select on ND, a random 10 percent of train where two thirds of the images have a partner, like val. Leak-free and fit sets are scored alongside as diagnostics. Every decision is screened on one seed and decided on the mean of three; the winner is retrained on all of train and uploaded. Note that one mAP point is worth almost four F1 points in delta-m.
