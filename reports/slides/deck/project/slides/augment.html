(~55 s) Our first addition is augmentation that follows radar physics. Speckle is multiplicative: an L-look image is the scene times Gamma noise of mean one. So we multiply each training image by fresh speckle with between 4 and 16 looks, as if the satellite had revisited the scene, and apply a random gamma, as if the image came from another source dataset’s intensity mapping. Neither moves a pixel. Over three seeds this gained 2.3 to 2.9 delta-m. The generic geometric augmentations did the opposite: zoom and mosaic lost about five points, because they teach invariance while the leaderboard rewards recognising repeated scenes as they are.
