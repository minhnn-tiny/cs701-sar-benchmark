(~45 s) Here are six random holdout images. Aircraft, cars, oil tanks and wind turbines are found, even the tiny turbines. Bridges and playgrounds are mostly missed, not mislocated: fewer than half are found even at a loose overlap threshold. Their boxes are as small as wind turbines’, but they sit in cluttered scenes where similar structures carry no box, and few of them repeat training scenes. A finer feature level and pasting their boxes into other images did not fix this. It is the clearest direction for future work. How we would solve it: condition the detection head on the scene class the classifier predicts, so it expects bridges in bridge scenes; give the stride-8 features more context, for example with attention; and mine look-alike structures, such as river crossings without a box, as hard negatives.
