input: a gray SAR image

output: class “car” + every car

Not a photo: bright = metal and corners, dark = water and runways, grain = speckle

Nine scene classes

aircraft · airport · bridge · car · harbor · oil tank · playground · ship · wind turbine

Budget

≤ 130 M parameters, ≤ 40 M trained; one model, one pass

Score: Δm

mean gain in macro-F1 and mAP over ViT-B/16 + LoRA; best in-budget baseline +4.4 val, +3.8 test

CS701 Team 5 · SAR multi-task learning · 2 / 17

(~50 s) Each image gets two answers: which of nine scene classes it is, and a box for every object, all of that class. Radar images are not photos: a pixel is echo strength, so metal and corners are bright, water and runways dark, and everything is grainy with speckle. The rules: one model, at most 130 million parameters with 40 million trained, one pass per image. The score, delta-m, is the average relative gain in macro-F1 and mAP over the course's ViT plus LoRA reference. The best baseline inside the budget reaches +4.4 on val.
