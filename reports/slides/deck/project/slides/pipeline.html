Our pipeline: one trunk, two heads, cross-task decoding

Orange: what we added or changed. Inference is one forward pass of one model.

CS701 Team 5 · SAR multi-task learning · 6 / 15

(~50 s) Here is the whole model. A gray SAR image goes into a COCO-pretrained YOLO26-L trunk, all 26 million parameters trained. The trunk feeds YOLO26’s detection heads and a tiny classification head of 5,600 parameters on the deepest feature. The orange parts are ours: SAR photometric augmentation during training, cross-task decoding at inference, where the detector refines the class and the class re-ranks the boxes, and weight averaging after training. I will go through the model choice, the augmentation and the decoding in turn.
