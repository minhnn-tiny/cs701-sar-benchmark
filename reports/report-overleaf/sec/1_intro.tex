\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Synthetic aperture radar (SAR) images the ground day and night and through clouds.
Its images are not photographs: a pixel records the echo of the radar's own pulse, so metal and corners shine as bright
points, water and runways stay dark, and coherent imaging covers uniform areas with multiplicative
speckle~\cite{argenti2013speckle}.
Our task asks one model with one shared backbone for two outputs per image: its scene class (aircraft, airport,
bridge, car, harbor, oil tank, playground, ship or wind turbine) and every object as a box carrying that class.
The model must stay within 130\,M parameters, at most 40\,M trained, and is scored by $\Delta_m$, the mean relative
gain in macro-F1 and COCO mAP over a ViT-B/16\,+\,LoRA reference~\cite{maninis2019attentive,vandenhende2021mtl}.

We studied the data before choosing a method, and three properties set our objectives.
\emph{Objects are tiny:} half of the 32{,}533 training boxes are smaller than one 16\,px ViT patch, and four classes
have median boxes of 8--15\,px.
A ViT-B/16 sees them through one stride-16 token map, and its LoRA variants lose 10--20\,\% mAP to full fine-tuning,
which is over the budget.
\emph{Scenes repeat:} the images come from four SAR datasets~\cite{li2024sardet,zhang2025rsar,wang2025m4sar,wei2020hrsid}
that share scenes, and 66.5\,\% of a random 10\,\% of training images have a copy, overlapping crop or revisit in the
rest; val scores behave as if val images do too.
The leaderboard therefore rewards fitting training scenes as much as generalising, and a leak-free holdout ranks
methods differently from val.
\emph{The tasks are coupled:} every box has its image's class, so each head carries evidence for the other.

Our objectives and contributions follow from these properties:
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item Spend the budget on resolution and detection design: a shared-trunk multi-task model on COCO-pretrained
      YOLO26-L~\cite{jocher2026yolo26}, 26.2\,M parameters, all trained, with a 5.6\,k-parameter classifier
      (\cref{sec:model}).
\item Validate on a holdout that mirrors val, with leak-free and fitting diagnostics, and decide on three seeds over
      more than 40 holdout runs (\cref{sec:protocol,sec:ablation}).
\item Train with SAR physics, not generic geometry: speckle and gamma noise gain +2.3 to +2.9 $\Delta_m$, where zoom
      and mosaic lose 4.6--5.4 (\cref{sec:aug}).
\item Let the heads correct each other at inference without parameters: cross-task decoding gains
      +0.61\,$\pm$\,0.15 $\Delta_m$ on nine of nine runs (\cref{sec:decoding}).
\end{itemize}
The final single model, with SWA~\cite{izmailov2018swa}, scores $\Delta_m$ +45.9 on val and +48.7 on test: over ten
times the best in-budget baseline (+4.4 / +3.8) and above over-budget full fine-tuning (+11.8 / +13.5).
